# Framework Mapping Matrix

This document provides a complete mapping of every policy template in this repository to the applicable SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (TSC) and ISO 27001:2022 Annex A controls.

Use this matrix to demonstrate auditor coverage and identify any gaps in your control environment. For a comprehensive readiness assessment, use TrazTech's free [SOC 2 Readiness Checklist](https://traztech.ca/soc-2-readiness-checklist).

## Notes for Auditors

- This mapping covers the most directly relevant controls per policy. Some policies provide supplementary evidence for controls primarily addressed by other policies.
- The mapping reflects SOC 2 2017 Trust Services Criteria and ISO 27001:2022 Annex A controls.
- For implementation guidance beyond policy documentation, see TrazTech's blog posts on [control drift between audits](https://traztech.ca/blog/control-drift-between-audits) and [who owns compliance after readiness](https://traztech.ca/blog/who-owns-compliance-after-readiness).
