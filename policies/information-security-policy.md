## 1. Purpose

This Information Security Policy establishes the foundation of [COMPANY_NAME]'s Information Security Management System (ISMS). It defines the organization's commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets, sets the strategic direction for information security, and provides the framework under which all subordinate security policies operate.

This policy exists because security is not a feature: it is an operational requirement. [COMPANY_NAME] handles data entrusted to us by customers, partners, and employees. Protecting that data is both a business obligation and an ethical responsibility.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- **All personnel:** Full-time and part-time employees, contractors, consultants, temporary workers, interns, and any individual granted access to [COMPANY_NAME] information systems or data.
- **All information assets:** Data in any form (electronic, paper, verbal), including customer data, intellectual property, financial records, employee records, and operational data.
- **All systems and infrastructure:** Production and non-production environments, cloud services, SaaS applications, endpoints, network infrastructure, and any system that processes, stores, or transmits [COMPANY_NAME] data.
- **All locations:** Corporate offices, remote work environments, coworking spaces, and any location where [COMPANY_NAME] business is conducted.

## 3. Information Security Objectives

[COMPANY_NAME] commits to the following security objectives:

1. **Protect customer data** as the highest priority, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability consistent with contractual commitments and regulatory requirements.
2. **Maintain a risk-based security program** that identifies, assesses, and treats information security risks proportionate to their potential business impact.
3. **Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations**, including but not limited to data protection regulations, industry standards, and customer agreements.
4. **Build security into operations** by embedding security controls into business processes, system development, and change management rather than treating security as an afterthought.
5. **Foster a security-aware culture** through ongoing training, clear communication, and leadership accountability.
6. **Continuously improve** the ISMS through regular assessments, audits, incident analysis, and management review.

## 4. Risk Appetite Statement

[COMPANY_NAME] accepts that all business activity involves risk. The organization maintains a low risk appetite for:

- Unauthorized disclosure of customer data
- Regulatory non-compliance
- Extended service unavailability affecting customer operations
- Insider threats involving privileged access abuse

The organization maintains a moderate risk appetite for:

- Adoption of new technologies that have been assessed through the risk management process
- Temporary operational disruptions during planned maintenance or upgrades

All risks exceeding the organization's appetite must be escalated to leadership and formally treated through the risk management process defined in the Risk Management Policy.

## 5. Policy Statements

### 5.1 Security Governance

- [COMPANY_NAME] shall maintain an Information Security Management System (ISMS) that is proportionate to the organization's size, complexity, and risk profile.
- Executive leadership shall demonstrate commitment to information security by allocating adequate resources, defining security roles, and participating in management reviews.
- A designated security lead (CISO, Head of Security, or equivalent) shall be responsible for the day-to-day management of the ISMS and shall report to executive leadership on security posture, risks, and incidents.
- The ISMS shall be reviewed by leadership at least annually, or more frequently when warranted by significant changes or incidents.

### 5.2 Policy Framework

- This policy serves as the top-level document in [COMPANY_NAME]'s security policy hierarchy. All subordinate policies, standards, and procedures must be consistent with this policy.
- Subordinate policies include but are not limited to: Access Control, Acceptable Use, Change Management, Incident Response, Data Classification, Vendor Management, Business Continuity, Encryption, Human Resources Security, Logging and Monitoring, Physical Security, and Risk Management.
- All security policies shall be reviewed at least annually and updated to reflect changes in the threat landscape, technology environment, regulatory requirements, or business operations.
- Approved policies shall be communicated to all relevant personnel and made readily accessible.

### 5.3 Asset Management

- [COMPANY_NAME] shall maintain an inventory of information assets, including data stores, applications, infrastructure components, and third-party services.
- Each information asset shall have a designated owner responsible for its classification, protection, and lifecycle management.
- Information assets shall be classified according to the Data Classification Policy and protected with controls appropriate to their classification level.

### 5.4 Compliance

- [COMPANY_NAME] shall identify and document all applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and industry requirements related to information security.
- Compliance with these requirements shall be monitored through internal assessments, external audits, and continuous control monitoring.
- Non-compliance shall be treated as a risk and addressed through the risk management process with defined remediation timelines.

### 5.5 Continuous Improvement

- The effectiveness of the ISMS shall be measured through key performance indicators (KPIs) including but not limited to: incident response times, vulnerability remediation timelines, policy compliance rates, training completion rates, and audit findings.
- Lessons learned from security incidents, near misses, and audit findings shall be incorporated into the ISMS.
- Management reviews shall assess the continued suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the ISMS and direct improvements where needed.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Executive Leadership (CEO/Board)** | Set the security tone from the top; approve the Information Security Policy; allocate budget and resources for the security program; participate in annual ISMS management review. |
| **Security Lead (CISO or equivalent)** | Manage the day-to-day ISMS; maintain the policy framework; oversee risk assessments and incident response; report to leadership on security posture; coordinate audits and compliance activities. |
| **Engineering/IT Leadership** | Implement technical security controls; ensure security requirements are incorporated into system design and development; maintain infrastructure security. |
| **People Operations/HR** | Administer background checks, onboarding/offboarding security procedures, and security awareness training; enforce the Acceptable Use Policy. |
| **All Employees and Contractors** | Comply with all security policies; complete required security training; report security incidents and suspected vulnerabilities; protect company and customer data in their custody. |
| **Policy Owners** | Maintain and review assigned policies; ensure policies reflect current operations; coordinate updates and approvals. |

## 7. Enforcement

Violations of this policy or any subordinate security policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract. Violations that involve criminal activity may be referred to law enforcement.

Employees who become aware of policy violations are required to report them to [SECURITY_TEAM_EMAIL] or through the organization's anonymous reporting mechanism.

## 8. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy or any subordinate security policy must be:

1. Requested in writing with a documented business justification
2. Approved by the Security Lead and the relevant policy owner
3. Time-limited (maximum 12 months, subject to renewal)
4. Documented in the risk register with compensating controls identified
5. Reviewed at each policy review cycle

## 10. Review Cadence

This policy shall be reviewed **annually** or upon:

- Significant organizational changes (mergers, acquisitions, major restructuring)
- Major security incidents
- Changes to applicable laws, regulations, or contractual obligations
- Significant changes to the technology environment
- Findings from internal or external audits
