## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy defines the rules and expectations for how [COMPANY_NAME] personnel use company-provided information systems, networks, devices, and data. The goal is to protect [COMPANY_NAME] and its customers from harm caused by misuse of technology resources, whether intentional or accidental, while enabling employees to work effectively.

This policy is not about restricting productivity. It is about ensuring that the tools and access we provide are used responsibly, and that everyone understands the boundaries.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees, contractors, consultants, temporary workers, and interns of [COMPANY_NAME]
- All company-owned or company-managed devices, including laptops, mobile phones, tablets, and peripherals
- All company-provided accounts, including email, SaaS applications, cloud platforms, code repositories, and communication tools
- Personal devices used to access [COMPANY_NAME] systems or data (BYOD)
- All network resources, including corporate Wi-Fi, VPN connections, and cloud infrastructure

## 3. Policy Statements

### 3.1 General Use and Ownership

- [COMPANY_NAME] information systems are provided primarily for business purposes. Limited personal use is acceptable provided it does not interfere with job responsibilities, consume excessive resources, or violate any provision of this policy.
- All data created, stored, sent, or received on company systems is the property of [COMPANY_NAME]. The company reserves the right to monitor, access, review, and disclose any information on company systems to the extent permitted by applicable law.
- Users should have no expectation of privacy when using company-provided systems, except as required by law.

### 3.2 Acceptable Activities

The following activities are acceptable when performed in the course of legitimate business:

- Accessing, creating, and managing data necessary for job functions
- Using approved collaboration tools (email, messaging, video conferencing) for business communications
- Installing software approved by IT or listed in the company's approved software catalog
- Accessing the internet for work-related research, professional development, and reasonable personal use during non-work periods
- Using company VPN to securely access internal resources from remote locations
- Connecting personal mobile devices to guest Wi-Fi networks where provided

### 3.3 Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on [COMPANY_NAME] systems and networks:

**Security Violations:**
- Sharing account credentials with any other person, including colleagues
- Attempting to bypass or disable security controls, including endpoint protection, MFA, or network security tools
- Connecting unauthorized devices to the corporate network (excluding guest Wi-Fi)
- Installing unauthorized software, browser extensions, or utilities that have not been approved by IT
- Accessing systems, data, or network segments beyond what is required for job responsibilities
- Running port scanners, vulnerability scanners, or penetration testing tools against company or third-party systems without explicit written authorization from the Security Lead
- Deliberately introducing malware, ransomware, or any malicious code

**Data Handling Violations:**
- Transferring company data classified as Confidential or Restricted to personal accounts, personal devices, or unapproved cloud storage services
- Sending customer data, source code, or internal documents to personal email addresses
- Posting company confidential information on social media, forums, or public repositories
- Disabling or circumventing data loss prevention (DLP) controls

**Unacceptable Content and Conduct:**
- Accessing, downloading, or distributing illegal content
- Using company systems for harassment, discrimination, or intimidation
- Using company email or systems to send unsolicited bulk messages (spam)
- Engaging in activities that could bring [COMPANY_NAME] into legal liability or reputational harm
- Using company resources for personal commercial ventures or outside employment without written approval

**Network and Infrastructure:**
- Operating unauthorized network services (DHCP servers, DNS servers, wireless access points)
- Deliberately degrading system or network performance
- Cryptocurrency mining using company resources
- Torrenting or peer-to-peer file sharing using company networks

### 3.4 Email and Communication

- Business email shall be the official communication channel for formal company business, contractual discussions, and communications with external parties.
- Employees shall not open suspicious attachments or click links in unsolicited emails. Suspected phishing attempts must be reported to [SECURITY_TEAM_EMAIL].
- Auto-forwarding of company email to external addresses is prohibited.
- Employees must use approved communication platforms for internal discussions involving sensitive or confidential information. Public or consumer-grade messaging apps (personal WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.) shall not be used for business discussions involving company data.

### 3.5 Password and Authentication

- Users must comply with the Access Control Policy regarding password complexity, uniqueness, and multi-factor authentication requirements.
- Passwords must not be written down, stored in plain text, or shared via email or messaging.
- Users must use the company-approved password manager for storing and generating credentials.
- Devices must be locked (screen lock) when unattended, with automatic lock set to a maximum of 5 minutes of inactivity.

### 3.6 Remote Work and Mobile Devices

- Employees working remotely must use company VPN or approved zero-trust access methods when accessing internal systems.
- Company devices must have full-disk encryption enabled at all times.
- Employees must not use public or untrusted Wi-Fi networks to access company systems without an active VPN connection.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to [SECURITY_TEAM_EMAIL] within 1 hour of discovery. All company devices must have remote wipe capability enabled.
- Employees must not leave company devices unattended in public spaces (airports, cafes, coworking spaces) or in vehicles.

### 3.7 Software and Cloud Services

- Only software and cloud services approved by IT may be used for company business. Shadow IT (unapproved SaaS tools, cloud accounts, or services) is prohibited.
- Employees who identify a business need for new software must submit a request through the IT procurement process.
- All software must be kept up to date. Employees must not defer or disable operating system or application updates beyond the timeline specified by IT.
- Browser extensions must be from the approved list. Employees must not install extensions that request access to browsing data, credentials, or page content without IT approval.

### 3.8 Social Media

- Employees may use personal social media on personal devices during non-work periods.
- Employees must not post company confidential information, proprietary code, customer data, security configurations, or internal communications on social media.
- Official [COMPANY_NAME] social media accounts may only be managed by authorized personnel.
- When discussing [COMPANY_NAME] publicly, employees must make clear they are expressing personal opinions, not official company positions (unless authorized to do so).

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **All Users** | Read, understand, and comply with this policy; complete security awareness training; report violations and suspicious activity to [SECURITY_TEAM_EMAIL]. |
| **Managers** | Ensure direct reports are aware of and comply with this policy; address violations through the disciplinary process; approve access requests appropriate to job roles. |
| **IT/Engineering** | Maintain the approved software catalog; enforce technical controls supporting this policy (endpoint management, DLP, network security); respond to reported violations. |
| **Security Lead** | Review and update this policy; investigate reported violations; recommend disciplinary action for serious violations; maintain monitoring and enforcement capabilities. |
| **HR** | Ensure all new hires acknowledge this policy during onboarding; coordinate disciplinary action for violations; manage offboarding to ensure return of assets and revocation of access. |

## 5. Monitoring and Enforcement

[COMPANY_NAME] reserves the right to monitor use of company systems, including but not limited to:

- Email content and metadata
- Web browsing activity
- File access and transfer activity
- Application usage
- Network traffic
- Endpoint security agent telemetry

Monitoring is conducted to protect [COMPANY_NAME] assets and data, ensure compliance with this policy, and support incident investigations. Monitoring activities are proportionate and conducted in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

## 6. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Verbal or written warning
- Mandatory additional security training
- Temporary or permanent restriction of system access
- Termination of employment or contract
- Civil or criminal prosecution, where applicable

The severity of disciplinary action will be proportionate to the nature, impact, and intent of the violation. Repeated minor violations will be treated with escalating severity.

## 7. Policy Acknowledgment

All [COMPANY_NAME] personnel must acknowledge this policy in writing (or electronically) upon hire and annually thereafter. Acknowledgment confirms that the individual has read, understands, and agrees to comply with this policy.

## 8. Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing to [SECURITY_TEAM_EMAIL] with a business justification and must be approved by the Security Lead. Exceptions are time-limited and documented in the risk register.

## 9. Review Cadence

This policy shall be reviewed **annually** or upon significant changes to the technology environment, workforce structure, or regulatory landscape.
